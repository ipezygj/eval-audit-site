Astrophysics & physics

MNRAS 537, 2313 (2025) · Fermi-GBM

Kilohertz QPOs in three short GRBs, reported at p < 10⁻¹⁰.

Rebuilt the full search from public GBM photon data; the three peaks reproduce. Against a null that keeps each burst's own light-curve shape (instead of a constant rate), the same search matches or beats the observed statistic in 89%, 40% and 4.9% of simulations.

not significant under a burst-shaped null

Correspondence with the authors open; methods paper in preparation (A&A).

ApJL 983 (2025) · CHIME

A possible ~126-day period in FRB 20240209A.

The published bootstrap tests whether burst times are random — not whether they are periodic. With the burst storms kept intact, the period can be neither confirmed nor refuted from three cycles: a power test with a truly periodic source shows the folding statistic detects it in only 5–7% of trials.

not decidable from these data

The author engaged in detail, confirmed a duplicate burst in the table, and plans an out-of-sample test as monitoring grows — which both sides agree is the test that matters.

MNRAS 540, 545 (2025) · BAO compilation

A local void preferred over Planck-ΛCDM at Δχ² = 28.5 across 42 "independent" BAO points.

The 42 points contain the same galaxy samples two to four times, and the Eisenstein–Hu sound horizon is converted for some points but not others. With one measurement per sample and a free H0·r_d, ΛCDM fits the paper's own data better than the void profile.

independence assumption fails

The authors' own follow-up paper (Universe 2026) independently reached the free-H0·r_d point; the second author shared the original working spreadsheet, which confirms this compilation (33 of 35 non-DESI values within 0.3%).

2025 preprint · H(z) compilation

A 6.4σ descending trend in H0 with redshift.

The supernova points are calibrated on SH0ES and the BAO points on the Planck sound horizon, so the "trend" restates the H0 tension between the two anchors. With calibrations included as uncertainties: 2.3σ; with them free: 0σ.

trend = tension, restated

Manuscript submitted to A&A (Sep 2026); code and data: doi:10.5281/zenodo.23002508.

2025 preprint · Kuiper belt

Orbital clustering evidence for an undiscovered planet.

The signal reproduces only with one shape parameter frozen; profiled over it the significance is 2.6σ / 2.4σ, the binning choice carries a global p of 0.20, and eleven newly catalogued objects go against the clustering (P = 0.03).

below discovery-grade once profiled

Shared with the lead author; a research note is planned.

2026 preprint · LHC combinations

A 152 GeV scalar at 5.4σ.

With the flat WW* background treated consistently and the search's own look-elsewhere effect (~13 effective trials), the combination drops to roughly 4.4σ, and one channel's status changes between the 2023 and 2025 datasets.

overstated; parked until journal publication

Earth & life sciences

JGR Oceans (2026) · coral palaeoclimate

Typhoon rainfall reconstruction correlates with AMO (r = 0.54) and solar activity (r = −0.48).

Both series are 11-year running means; the effective sample size is 6–8, and phase-randomised surrogates give p = 0.33 and 0.42. The unsmoothed annual correlations are ±0.3 and not significant.

smoothing artefact

The first author confirmed the published p-values used the nominal number of years with no autocorrelation adjustment, and described the correlations as low-frequency covariability.
